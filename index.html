The Lunar method

One setup, six steps, written down so you can test it.

  1. Read the higher timeframeTrade with the H1 or H4 structure. Sells in a bearish higher-timeframe trend, buys in a bullish one. Counter-trend trades get a lower grade.
  2. Map fresh liquidity (MSNR)Mark swing highs and lows price has not revisited. Equal highs or lows are where stops cluster.
  3. Wait for the sweepPrice wicks through the level and closes back inside. A wick of at least a quarter ATR is a cleaner sweep than a tiny poke.
  4. Confirm the shiftA close beyond the last swing with a body of 1× ATR or more. Weak shifts are skipped.
  5. Enter on the retestUse the fair value gap or order block, ideally overlapping the 62–79% retracement. Stop beyond the sweep extreme.
  6. Take profit before the poolTargets sit just short of untouched liquidity. Close 50% at TP1 and move the stop to breakeven, then 30% and 20%.

Rules that protect you

Risk 0.5–1% per trade. One setup at a time. No entries within minutes of high-impact US data. Stop after two losses in a day. Skip any trade below 1:1.5 to TP1. A high grade raises the odds a little; it never guarantees a win.
